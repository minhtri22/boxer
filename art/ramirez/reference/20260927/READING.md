## Current visible differences to correct

- The reference has a tucked chin, forward readiness, compact guard and clear
  staggered feet. The current study is too upright through the head/neck.
- Reference gloves have a curled finger/palm silhouette, distinct thumb and
  worn red leather. Current gloves are too oval and their seams protrude.
- Reference wraps surround and follow the forearm. The current wrap was attached
  rigidly to the hand and penetrated the forearm during wrist flexion.
- Reference shoulders, upper back and upper arms are fuller, with connected
  anatomical forms. This must be sculpted locally, not achieved by stretching
  the whole fighter along an axis or changing the arm-bone lengths.
- Reference face, brow, hair clumps and beard silhouette differ materially from
  the current generic face. Shader changes alone cannot establish likeness.
- Reference skin has varied diffuse detail and restrained highlights; the
  procedural study has a wax/plastic appearance.
- Reference shorts have structured folds, a wider name panel and shorter legs;
  the current oversized smooth garment and fine jagged trim still need work.
- Reference boots expose more calf, have a slimmer ankle shaft, red inserts and
  worn lace/toe details. Current shafts are too tall and simplified.

No visual PASS is inferred from these observations. A source model, if supplied,
should be inspected for reuse before spending more budget rebuilding its detail.
