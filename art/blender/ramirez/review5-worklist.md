## V5 work items

1. Rebuild the trunks silhouette so the leg shells follow the hips at the top and flare naturally toward the hem.
2. Keep separate left/right leg openings while making the lower hem visibly non-rigid through controlled drape and a real outer side split.
3. Keep fabric thin and soften the waistband/side treatment so the garment does not read as a hard shell.
4. Lower the boxing base further: knees flexed, pelvis lower, torso slightly forward, chin tucked, shoulders active.
5. Tighten neutral guard: gloves closer to cheek line and elbows protecting the ribs.
6. Make the jab body-supported: lead shoulder travels with the hand, rear hand stays home, stance stays grounded.
7. Make the cross visibly driven from the rear side: rear-foot pivot, knee/hip drive, pelvis/shoulder rotation and weight transfer must all read in silhouette.
8. Rebuild slips from the floor upward: asymmetric knee load + pelvis shift + torso angle move the head off center while both feet remain planted.
9. Rebuild slip-counter as a loaded evade followed by rear-side drive; avoid the “lean and extend arm” silhouette.
10. Recover to the same compact guard rather than an upright reset.
11. Render Review5 static, combat and clay evidence using the same non-hiding review camera set.
12. Run machine QA, then review the evidence manually against the approved reference before issuing a final status.
