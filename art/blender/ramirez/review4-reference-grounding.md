## Locked pose constraints

- Neutral guard: forward crouch, chin tucked, active shoulders, elbows protecting torso, staggered grounded base.
- Jab: lead extension, rear hand guarding, lead shoulder contributing to chin protection, no arm-only reach.
- Cross: rear-hand extension with lead hand guarding, hip/shoulder rotation, rear-foot pivot and visible weight transfer.
- Slip left/right: head exits centerline through knees/hips/torso participation while both feet remain grounded.
- Slip + counter: evade position remains structurally supported while the rear-side kinetic chain drives the counter.
- Recover: return to the same stable guard rather than an upright reset.
