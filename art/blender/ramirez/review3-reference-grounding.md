## Pose constraints applied

- Stance: staggered base, flexed knees, one side leading, stable balance.
- Guard: slight crouch, chin tucked, shoulders active, elbows in.
- Jab: compact lead-hand extension with rear hand retained and modest hip contribution.
- Cross: rear-side punch with rear-foot pivot, hip/shoulder rotation, weight transfer, lead hand retained near face.
- Slip left/right: head and upper torso leave center line with level change while guard remains meaningful.
- Slip + counter: head stays off center while the rear-side kinetic chain rotates into the counter and the other hand remains useful guard.
- Recovery: return to the same stable guard/base.

This file records pose grounding only. Human visual approval remains the authority for the Blender gate.
