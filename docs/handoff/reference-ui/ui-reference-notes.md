# Boxer — Approved UI/UX Reference Notes

## Shared art direction
- realistic/stylized high-fidelity 3D boxing;
- premium black / warm gold / controlled red accents;
- cinematic arena or gym lighting;
- strong mobile contrast;
- bold condensed sports typography;
- first-person gloves as framing where appropriate;
- opponent remains primary readable threat;
- aspirational discipline/growth/championship tone.

## 01 Character customization
Future surface: fighter preview; name; nationality; skin; hair; facial hair; gloves; trunks; wraps; fighting style; strong gold CONFIRM CTA.

## 02 Home / main menu
Logo + `Fight from your own eyes`; teach Phone=Head, Left Thumb=Feet, Right Thumb=Punch, No Touch=Guard; strong START; secondary Training/Career; POV gloves foreground.

## 03 POV combat HUD — immediate UAT authority
Top-left player identity/HP/stamina; top-center round/timer; top-right opponent identity/HP/stamina; opponent centered; player gloves foreground; movement bottom-left; guard bottom-center; punch zone bottom-right; premium arena lighting.

## 04 Venues / career
Future surface: venue progression, rewards/rank, Next Fight CTA. Visual lock only; no early implementation authorization.

## 05 Training / coach
Future surface: Head Movement, Footwork, Punch Mechanics, Guard, Conditioning, with coach anchor and Train Now CTA.

## 06 Ramirez turnaround
Production opponent authority: muscular middleweight, compact guard, dark hair/beard, red gloves, red/gold trunks, white/red boots, readable multi-angle silhouette. Rig must preserve validated chains `shoulder → upper arm → elbow joint → forearm → glove` and `pelvis → thigh → knee joint → shin → foot`.

## Debug policy
UAT may retain diagnostics behind a toggle/dev build, but user-facing presentation must not be dominated by debug text, traces, gizmos or placeholder blocks.
